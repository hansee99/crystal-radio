Quietening the volume slider
You're right that it's too loud. The cause isn't really the width — it's that volume is styled identically to the seek bar: same teal fill, same thumb, same track height, on its own full row. That says "these two controls matter equally," but seek is touched constantly and volume is set once a session. Three options below, live — drag any slider, hover the hidden ones.

Whichever you pick
Give volume a neutral track — #8A9F9A fill on rgba(255,255,255,0.14), 3px tall, 10px thumb. Teal is your "this is live / this is the thing" colour; spending it on a volume slider is what makes it shout. The seek bar keeps teal, 4px, and a 12px thumb, so the two stop looking like siblings. Keep the numeric readout on hover/drag only — a permanently visible "100" is telling the user a number they set themselves.
