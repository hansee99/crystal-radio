Crystal Radio · UX & design audit

Three modes, one player, three different rulebooks

The skeleton works — the left-panel/Now-Playing split is the right shape for a long-session player, and the glowing play button plus the LIVE-badge/equalizer pairing give the app a genuine signature. The problems are almost all downstream of that: the right panel's artwork is doing damage to legibility, DJ mode is running five states through one status line, and Library's row templates have drifted far enough from Radio's that they read as two different apps.

The five things I'd fix first
01
Text is sitting on raw artwork
Every contrast number in your token file is measured against #050D0C. On the right panel that background is a bright cream-and-teal bokeh. The recently-played list, the seek times and the transport row all cross it.
02
DJ mode's status line is overloaded
Idle, scouting, warming up, steady and refilling all render as one 11.5px muted sentence with the same spinner — while the panel below it still says "Not harvesting yet."
03
The sparkle means two things
Your tokens say sparkle = "AI does something here." In Radio history and Library it's also the save-this-song button, and it's on the DJ Stop button, which is not an AI action.
04
Raw stream metadata is reaching the 44px title
We Gotta Get Out Of This Place ("Stereo") ( / 1965) — the empty-field artifact and quoted mix name are rendered at the app's largest, most confident type size.
05
The two dialogs aren't in the design system at all
About and Options use default WPF chrome and light gray OK / Cancel / Save buttons, with no scrim behind them. After eighteen screens of a carefully toned dark UI they land like a different application — and they're the two places a new user goes first.
01

First impressions, screen by screen

Radio · Stations
Stopped, playing, row hover

Reads clearly: what's playing, which station it came from, and that it's live. The station chip under the title is a smart, quiet piece of provenance. The equalizer swapping into the selected row's icon tile is the best state cue in the app.

Doesn't: in the stopped state the 44px slot holds the station name and the subtitle holds a description truncated mid-word — "listener-su…" — that is already fully visible in the left list two inches away. The lone AAC chip is unlabelled and at the faintest text tier, so it reads as debris. The row-hover edit/delete buttons appear only on hover with no other affordance, and the bottom-left + is stranded 500px below the list it adds to.

Radio · Search
In progress, results

Reads clearly: results are honest and plainly written — "Found 6 stations you can play" is exactly the right voice, and per-row descriptions make the picks defensible.

Doesn't: during the search, an operation you say can run 30+ seconds is represented by a 14px spinner and one faint line in the top-left corner, with ~420px of empty panel below it. Nothing communicates duration, nothing can be cancelled, and if it fails there is no visible place for the failure to go. Show different is ambiguous — different results, different sort, or a new search?

Radio · History
Song list, About this track

Reads clearly: the About panel is the most polished screen in the set. THE SONG / THE ARTIST eyebrows, the measured line length, and the "AI-generated · may be imperfect" footer next to Regenerate are all exactly right.

Doesn't: in the history list, some rows carry a download glyph and others don't, with no legend — I read it as "saved vs not saved" but that's a guess, and it's conveyed by icon presence alone. Timestamps are clock-only (17:48), so a list spanning days is unreadable. In the About panel, body copy fades out at the bottom over the brightest part of the artwork — it looks like a rendering fault rather than a scroll hint.

Library
Curate ×3, Songs

Reads clearly: the CURATED PLAYLIST result card is a genuinely nice object — eyebrow, title, count, provenance — and it makes the AI feel like a feature rather than a chatbot.

Doesn't: the helper line "Describe a mood or vibe above…" floats at an arbitrary vertical position, and stays on screen while the spinner runs — telling the user to do the thing they just did. The playlist title echoes the prompt verbatim including the typo ("Ecclectic"), title-cased, which reads as a bug. And the Songs list mixes two row templates: some rows show artist, others show a three-line AI description and no artist at all. That single list breaks the row scale, the rhythm, and scannability at once.

DJ
Idle, warming up, steady, history

Reads clearly: "Tuned into 4 stations · 1 song in the mix" is the single best status string in the app — concrete, plainly worded, quietly proud. The harvesting list makes an invisible process visible, which is the right instinct.

Doesn't: the prompt placeholder is clipped mid-word — "…spins up a live statio". Start DJ session and Stop are identical teal pills of equal weight, both sparkled, with Stop enabled when there's nothing to stop. During warm-up the panel says "Not harvesting yet — start a session above" while a session is demonstrably starting, and Now Playing says "Not playing" even though the brief says the best station is live at that moment. Harvest rows read "0 song titles seen" — internal vocabulary, and a zero is the least reassuring number you can show someone waiting.

Dialogs
About, Options

Reads clearly: the Options content model is good — three honest sections, plain-language reassurance about the key, an explicit note about respecting station terms.

Doesn't: both dialogs are unthemed. Light system buttons, system title bars, system link blue, no scrim, no teal anywhere. About's second paragraph ("translating it into a directory search and ranking the results") is the most technical copy in the product, in the place most likely to be read by someone deciding whether they trust it.

02

Hierarchy & layout

The split itself is sound: dense-left / spacious-right is the correct model for an app you leave open for six hours. It does its job fully in Radio, partly in Library, and least well in DJ — where the right panel has the least to say precisely when the user most wants to know what's happening.

All modes
Now Playing artwork
High

The background image has a bright cream/white diagonal running through the lower-right third — exactly where the transport, volume slider, seek times and the tail of the recently-played list live. In the Radio playing screen, "Insomnia / Crooked Fingers · 9 hr ago" is effectively unreadable. The stop button and the volume value disappear into the wash. This one issue invalidates most of the contrast work in your token file.

Change to
Put a vertical scrim between the artwork and the content column: a left-to-right LinearGradientBrush from #F2050D0C at x=0 to #00050D0C at x=0.75, plus a bottom scrim #00050D0C → #E6050D0C over the lower 200px for the transport. The artwork keeps its atmosphere at the top-right where nothing sits on it.
Library, DJ
"Not playing" null state
Medium

"Not playing" is rendered at 44px — the same weight the app gives a song title — and then followed by roughly 300px of empty artwork before the transport. The loudest element on screen is an absence, and it offers no next step. It's also vertically centred differently in Library-Curate than in DJ-idle, so switching modes makes it jump.

Change to
Drop to 28px Light at the Secondary tier, anchored at the same baseline in all three modes, with a mode-specific second line at 15px Muted: Radio → "Pick a station on the left." · Library → "Play a saved song, or curate a set." · DJ → "Describe a vibe to start a session."
DJ · left panel
Five control rows before content
Medium

Mode pills → prompt → two buttons → status line → sub-tabs → list. Radio reaches its list in two rows; DJ takes five, pushing the harvest list ~95px lower than the equivalent list in every other mode. The status line is also stranded above the sub-tabs, so it floats between the controls and the content that it describes — which is how you end up with a "warming up" line directly above "Not harvesting yet."

Change to
Collapse rows 2–4 into one session block: prompt field with the primary action as a trailing button inside it, Stop demoted to a ghost button that only appears while a session runs, and the status moved inside the Harvesting panel where it replaces the empty-state line. Sub-tabs then sit at the same y as Radio's and Library's.
Radio · stopped
Station description in the title slot
Low

The 44px/20px pair is a song-title/artist template. Filling it with station-name/description means the 20px Light line gets a hard mid-word truncation, and the station chip below repeats the name a third time.

Change to
Keep the station name at 44px, replace the artist line with "Ready to play · AAC 128k" at Muted, and let the description wrap to two lines at 15px below the chip — or drop it entirely, since it's already in the row you just clicked.

All modes
Sparkle glyph overloaded
High

Your token doc lists the sparkle as a signature moment meaning "AI does something here." It currently also means save-this-song (Radio history rows, Now Playing recently-played rows) and appears on Stop, which stops a session rather than invoking anything. Meanwhile Radio's AI search — the most AI thing in the app — gets a plain magnifier. The one symbol you've invested in is diluted in three directions at once.

Change to
Sparkle = AI only (Search field, Curate, Start DJ session, About this track, Regenerate). Save-to-library gets its own glyph — a plus-in-circle, filled teal once saved. Stop loses the sparkle and becomes a ghost square/round button in the transport family. Radio's search field gains the sparkle.
Dialogs
Outside the theme entirely
High

System title bars, system-blue links, light gray OK / Cancel / Save, sharp corners, no scrim behind the dialog so the running app competes for attention. The Options text field is the only input in the product without the radius-10 hairline treatment.

Change to
One AppDialog shell: WindowStyle=None, #06110F surface, radius 12, hairline border, your TitleIconButton close, a #99050D0C scrim over the main window while open. Primary action = teal pill, secondary = ghost. Links use #56C7BA.
04

State feedback

Every async state in the app is rendered as the same object: a 14px spinner plus one 11.5px muted sentence, top-left of the panel. That's a fine component — it just can't carry a 45-second multi-stage operation, and there's no error variant of it anywhere in the eighteen screens.

Loading
Present everywhere, but undifferentiated. A 2-second curate and a 45-second DJ start look identical.
Empty
Three different treatments: centred helper paragraph (Library), top-left faint line (DJ), nothing at all (Radio Stations, Search before first use).
Error
Not visible in any screen. Stream failures, zero results, expired key, and DJ exhaustion have no designed home.
DJ · warming up
Two contradicting states on screen
High

"Warming up the decks — scouting stations for your vibe…" sits immediately above "Not harvesting yet — start a session above." Meanwhile Now Playing reads "Not playing," although the brief says the best station is bridging live at this moment. A user who reads the screen honestly concludes nothing is happening.

Change to
The empty state must never coexist with an active status. Replace the Harvesting panel contents with a staged checklist while warming up — ✓ Found 6 stations · ◐ Checking they play · ○ Recording the first songs — and give Now Playing the live bridge station with its LIVE badge plus a small "Bridging until the mix fills" line, so the right panel isn't lying.
Library · curating
Helper text persists during load
High

"Describe a mood or vibe above to curate a playlist from your recordings" stays visible while "Curating a playlist…" spins. Same class of bug as the DJ one: the empty state isn't bound to the same visibility condition as the content.

Change to
One PanelState enum per list panel — Empty | Loading | Content | Error — driving a single ContentControl. Mutually exclusive by construction, and it gives you the missing Error state for free in all six panels.
Radio · search · DJ · start
30–60s waits, no progress, no cancel
Medium

Rotating status copy is a good idea, but rotation alone doesn't signal progress — after 20 seconds the user can't tell the difference between "working" and "hung." There's also no way out of a search you've decided against.

Change to
Stage the waits: 3–4 named steps with completed ones ticked and left on screen. Add three skeleton rows below (radius-10, 34px tile, two shimmer bars) so the panel isn't empty. Put a ghost "Cancel" at the right end of the status row for anything expected to exceed ~5 seconds.
DJ · steady
Spinner still spinning at rest
Medium

"Tuned into 4 stations · 1 song in the mix" still carries the loading spinner. A spinner that never stops stops meaning anything — and steady state is the state the user will see for hours.

Change to
Swap the spinner for the pulsing teal dot you already use in the LIVE badge once the session is running. Reserve the spinner strictly for "you are waiting on something." Also worth surfacing the refill state here — "1 song in the mix" with nothing else said is quietly alarming.
Radio · history rows
Saved vs unsaved is unlabelled
Medium

Some rows show a download glyph, some don't; the difference is never explained, and saving produces no visible confirmation in the screens provided. Saving a song is the main bridge between Radio and Library — it deserves a state, not an inference.

Change to
Three explicit row states: unsaved (outline plus, visible on hover/focus), saving (spinner in place of the glyph), saved (filled teal check, always visible, tooltip "In your library"). No new components required.
05

Copy & tone

The voice is largely working — "Warming up the decks", "Found 6 stations you can play", "respect the stations' terms" all hit professional-but-quirky without straining. The failures cluster at the edges: internal vocabulary leaking into status lines, and the About dialog slipping into engineering register. One rule would fix most of it: never show the user a number or noun that only makes sense if you've read the source.

Pick one noun per concept
A station is a live stream. A song is a song, everywhere — not "track", not "recording", not "song title". Your saved songs are your library. A DJ run is a session; what it's collected is the mix. That gives you "About this song", "1 song in the mix", "in your library", "end session" — and you can delete "recordings", "titles seen", and "harvesting" from user-facing copy entirely. Theme.xaml
Faint tier fails at body sizes
High

Faint at 3.9:1 is below AA for anything under 18.66px, and it's currently carrying the 11.5–12px status lines, the history timestamps, the codec chip and the volume readout — small text, which needs more contrast, not less. It's a token change, so it propagates for free.

Change to
Lift Faint from #5F736F to #8A9F9A (≈7.1:1) and reserve the old value for non-text only — hairlines, disabled glyphs, decorative separators. The tonal relationship between the tiers is preserved; the bottom step just stops being decorative.
All modes
Hit targets under 32px
Medium

The 28×28 ghost squares (add station, row edit, row delete) sit below the Fluent 32px minimum, and edit/delete are adjacent with an ~8px gap — a mis-click deletes a station. The underline sub-tabs are text-only with no vertical padding, giving roughly a 16px tall target. The row save glyphs read as ~24px.

Change to
32×32 minimum on all icon buttons (keep the 13–20px glyph, grow the padding — nothing moves visually). Sub-tabs get Padding="2,8" for a 32px band. Delete gets a confirm, or an undo line in the status slot for 5 seconds.
All modes
Focus visuals & colour-alone cues
Medium

The only focus indicator visible anywhere is WPF's default dotted rectangle on the pause button — which is both ugly and inconsistent. For an app someone keeps open all day, keyboard operation deserves a real ring. Most state cues are honest (selected row = teal tint plus a 2px bar; playing = equalizer motion; LIVE = dot plus word), but two rely on colour alone: mode pill selection is tint-only aside from the border, and DJ session active/inactive is conveyed only by two identically-styled teal buttons.

Change to
One FocusVisualStyle in Theme.xaml: 2px #56C7BA ring, 3px offset, matching the control's radius; suppress the default dotted rect. Make the selected mode pill SemiBold as well as tinted, and let Start/Stop swap rather than sit side by side.

b · Worthwhile medium
New shared components, no skeleton change.
01One PanelState control (Empty / Loading / Content / Error) across all six list panels — kills two live bugs and supplies the missing error state.
02Staged progress + Cancel for the 30s+ AI operations, with skeleton rows instead of an empty panel.
03Two row templates only — 44px compact and 60px with description — applied to every list, ending Library's mixed rows.
04Theme both dialogs into the app shell, with a scrim and teal primary buttons.
05Reclaim the sparkle for AI; give save-to-library its own three-state glyph.
06Restructure DJ's left panel: session block, status inside the panel it describes, sub-tabs aligned with the other modes.
07Show the per-pick reason the brief promises on curated playlist rows.
08Content tab first in every mode: Stations / Search · Songs / Curate · Mix / Sources.
09Give the transport a defined surface strip so it never floats on bright artwork.
10Move "add station" into the Stations list header; retire the stranded bottom-left +.
11Promote the DJ intro line: 15px italic at Secondary in a bordered block, not 12.5px muted over artwork. It's the mode's signature and it's currently the hardest thing to read.
c · Bigger ideas
Worth knowing about even if deferred.
Make the DJ session a first-class object
A session card at the top of the panel — vibe, elapsed time, stations tuned, songs in the mix, refill health — replaces the status line entirely and gives all five states somewhere structural to live. Harvest rows become little live meters rather than counters.
Let the artwork respond to what's playing
One fixed image with a bright diagonal will fight every layout you put over it forever. Either derive a heavily-darkened blur from station/album art, or ship 3–4 ambient variants that rotate slowly — same calm, no hotspots.
One save model across all three modes
Right now saving exists only in Radio history. Every song surface — DJ history, curated queue, Now Playing — should offer the same affordance and the same confirmation. That's what makes the library feel like the centre of the app rather than a third tab.
Show what's next
Library and DJ both play from a queue but never show the next track in Now Playing. A single "Up next · Title — Artist" line under the transport would do it, and it's the thing a listener most often wants to know without switching panels.
Keyboard-first operation
For a hands-off, hours-long app: a defined tab order, space to play/pause, Ctrl+K to focus whichever prompt the current mode owns, Ctrl+S to save the playing song.
About this track as a rail, not a swap
Currently it hides Now Playing, so you lose sight of what's playing while reading about it. If the window can widen, a third column keeps both; if not, an inline expansion under the title would preserve context.
